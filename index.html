Important Safety Note

This website is an educational demo, not a verified donor service. Do not enter real personal information. Browser storage is not a secure database. Blood group alone does not establish transfusion compatibility. For real donations, contact a licensed blood bank or healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a hospital.
